Inside Talexa

An intelligence layer for every stage of hiring

From the first WhatsApp message to a ranked shortlist on your recruiter's desk — see how Talexa runs the first round end to end.

Live conversation database

Every WhatsApp conversation, updating live

Talexa holds the entire first-round conversation on WhatsApp — asking your questions and understanding replies in Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, English and more.

  • Thousands of candidate threads, searchable in one place
  • Collects CVs, CTC, notice period and more, automatically
  • 24/7 — no recruiter in the loop until the shortlist is ready
Candidate database

Every candidate, scored and searchable

Resumes are parsed the moment they arrive, and every applicant is ranked against your role in real time — so the best matches surface without manual sifting.

  • CTC, notice period, company and fit captured per candidate
  • Filter by status, phone, company or campaign instantly
  • One-click profile view with the full screening history
No-code flow builder

Design the screening flow — no code

A visual builder lets you design the whole WhatsApp recruitment journey — questions, capture fields, conditions, and AI decisions — or generate one with AI and launch in minutes.

  • Drag-and-drop nodes: questions, conditions, AI smart decisions
  • Branch on the candidate's own answers automatically
  • Start from a best-practice template or generate with AI

Interview scheduling & what data Talexa accesses

When a candidate qualifies, Talexa can schedule the interview for you. With your permission, it connects to your Google Calendar for one purpose only: to check your availability and create the interview event, so the meeting lands on both calendars without back-and-forth. Talexa does not read, sell, or repurpose your calendar data for anything else, and you can disconnect the integration at any time. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the full detail.
